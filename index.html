Shortcuts: Space pause, . step, R reseed, C clear, P next palette, S save image, 1/2 pick a tool, arrow keys on the map nudge the rates.

Two chemicals share every cell: A is fed in everywhere, B converts A into more of itself and is slowly removed. Because A spreads faster than B, small blobs of B grow at their edges and starve in the middle, and the feed and kill rates decide whether that makes spots, stripes, mazes or cells that divide.
